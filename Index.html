Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

Súil respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, usage analytics, or location data.

All gear entries and mileage logs are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure on-device storage so your app and Home Screen widget stay in sync.
